📱 Mobile Dominance

56.4%

According to Pew Research (via ReadyCloud, 2026), 91% of U.S. adults own a smartphone. eMarketer reports mobile devices accounted for 56.4% of online transactions during the 2025 holiday season.

Conclusions & Analysis
  • 1
    E-Commerce is Universal: 100% of participants have purchased online, aligning with national trends showing e-commerce as a deeply embedded behavior, with 98% of adults owning cellphones and many shopping online "almost constantly" (Pew Research).
  • 2
    Frequency Drives Revenue: The majority (50%) shop monthly. This "subscription-like" habit supports Adobe Analytics data showing consistent year-over-year growth (5.3% in Q4 2025), suggesting habitual online spending is now a primary retail channel.
  • 3
    The Amazon Effect: 70% preferring Amazon validates market research showing marketplaces growing 4.5× faster than traditional e-commerce due to convenience, Prime ecosystems, and trust signals (Mirakl, 2026).
  • 4
    Mobile is the Present: With 91% smartphone penetration and over half of holiday sales on mobile, the survey's demographic (predominantly 26–39) represents the core mobile-first shopping generation driving this shift.
  • 5
    Education Correlates with Online Activity: 50% of respondents being college graduates and 100% having purchased online mirrors national data showing higher-educated consumers adopt e-commerce at higher rates and spend more per transaction.

📌 Final Takeaway: Ecommerce is no longer an "alternative" shopping method but the primary retail channel for most consumers under 40, driven by mobile accessibility and marketplace convenience.
